# Etiquette and tips

Cards by place type. Each card has a severity: **must** (it causes real offense or trouble), **should**, or **nice**. Each has a one-line why. Area-specific cards are in `places.md`.

## Everywhere

- **must · No tipping.** Leaving cash confuses people, and staff may chase you to return it. Why: service is included; tipping isn't a custom. [N][T]
- **should · Pay on the tray.** Put cash or card on the small tray by the register, not in the cashier's hand. [T]
- **should · Carry your trash.** Public bins are rare. Konbini bins work for things you bought there. Why: there are few bins by design. [N][T][V]
- **should · Don't eat while walking.** Step aside and finish. It's not illegal, just reads as careless. Some streets (Nishiki) post signs asking you not to. [N][V]
- **should · Shoes off at any step up.** Entryways, ryokan, some restaurants and temple halls. Slippers wait at the step. Wear decent socks. [N]
- **nice · Keep perfume light.** Small spaces and crowded trains. The value at work is *meiwaku*: don't trouble the people around you. [N]
- **should · Smoke only in marked areas.** Street smoking is banned in big cities, with fines of about ¥1,000–2,000. Many small bars still allow smoking inside. [N]
- **nice · Drinking outside is legal.** Being loudly drunk is not welcome. [N]
- **must · Ignore street touts.** In Shinjuku and Shibuya at night, never follow anyone pulling you into a bar. Why: overcharging and pressure to pay. [T]
- **should · Carry tissues and a hand towel.** Some restrooms lack paper, and most lack paper towels. [N][T]
- **should · Use the restroom before leaving the gates.** Exiting means paying again to re-enter. [T]

## Restaurants and izakaya

- **must · Call the server.** Say "sumimasen!" when ready to order. They won't come until you do. [N]
- **must · Chopsticks.** Never stand them upright in rice, and never pass food chopstick to chopstick. Why: both echo funeral rites. [N]
- **should · Ask for the check.** The bill won't come on its own. Say "okaikei onegaishimasu", then pay at the register. [N][T]
- **should · Eat and go at busy places.** Ramen counters especially are built for turnover. Don't linger after eating. [T]
- **should · Use the ramen ticket machine.** Pick, pay, and hand the ticket to staff. Most buttons have photos. [N]
- **should · Expect a table charge (otoshi).** Izakaya often bring a small dish you didn't order and charge for it, about ¥300–600 each. It's normal, not a scam. [V]
- **nice · Order a few small plates to share.** Ask for the osusume (house picks). [N]
- **nice · Ask before you sit if smoke matters.** Old izakaya often allow smoking. [N]

## Sushi counter and omakase

- **must · Arrive on time.** Seatings start together. Late arrivals hold up the counter. [V]
- **must · Eat each piece right away.** The chef times the temperature of rice and fish to the moment it's served. [V]
- **should · Hands or chopsticks are both fine for nigiri.** Dip the fish side, not the rice, if you use soy sauce at all. Often the chef has already seasoned it. [V]
- **should · No strong perfume.** It competes with the food at a counter. [N][V]
- **should · Ask before taking photos.** Some top counters ban photos. [V]
- **nice · Say "gochisousama deshita" to the chef as you leave.** [N]

## Cocktail bars

- **should · Expect a seat charge.** Many small bars add a charge per person (often ¥500–1,500), sometimes with a snack. Ask if unsure: "chaaji wa arimasu ka?" [V]
- **should · Read the house rules.** Some bars set a seat limit, a one-drink minimum, no photos, or no standing groups. Many are up stairs or behind unmarked doors. [V]
- **should · Keep your voice down.** Many are 8 to 12 seats. [V]
- **nice · Order by mood.** "Osusume de" (your recommendation) works well. Say a spirit and "not too sweet". [V]

## Onsen

- **must · Wash before you soak.** Sit on the stool and scrub fully, then rinse the stool and turn the basin upside down. [N]
- **must · No swimsuit. Everything comes off.** [N][K]
- **must · Keep the small towel out of the water.** Fold it on your head or set it on the edge. The big towel stays in the locker or your room. [N]
- **must · No phones or cameras in the bath area.** This rule holds everywhere. [N]
- **must · Check the curtain.** Red or pink noren with 女 is women's. Blue with 男 is men's. The kanji are what count, not the color. Some ryokan swap sides at set times. [N]
- **should · Tattoos: ask ahead.** Hakone is more relaxed than most places. Private baths (kashikiri-buro) avoid the issue. [N]
- **should · Keep quiet and don't stare.** [N]
- **should · Dry off before the changing room.** Return borrowed towels and keys. [N]
- **should · Drink water.** Hot baths dehydrate you faster than you'd think. [N]

## Ryokan

- **must · Yukata left over right.** The other way is for dressing the dead. Tie the sash at your natural waist. Layer the heavier jacket (tanzen) over it, don't swap. [N]
- **must · No slippers on tatami.** Bare feet or socks only. [N]
- **must · Toilet slippers stay in the toilet.** Swap at the door, both ways. [N]
- **must · Dinner is at a set time.** Be on time. [N]
- **should · Arrive by check-in (about 3–5 PM).** That leaves time to settle and bathe before dinner. [N]
- **should · Let staff carry your bags and lay out the futon.** Don't rearrange the room. [N]
- **should · Have the welcome tea and sweets right away.** That's what they're for. [N]
- **nice · At checkout (usually by 10 AM), fold the yukata loosely and say "osewa ni narimashita".** [N]
- **nice · Best bath times are just before dinner or late at night.** [N]

## Shrines (torii gate, Shinto)

- **must · Wash at the water basin (temizuya).** Ladle in the right hand, rinse the left. Switch, rinse the right. Pour water into your left palm and rinse your mouth, but never touch the ladle to your lips. Tip the ladle upright to rinse the handle, then set it back. [T]
- **must · Pray: two bows, two claps, one bow.** Drop a coin in the offering box first. [T]
- **should · Walk at the side of the path.** The center is for the deity. [V]
- **should · Ask before photographing people praying.** [T]

## Temples (Buddhist)

- **must · No clapping.** Palms together, a quiet wish, a bow. [T]
- **must · Assume no photos inside halls** unless a sign says otherwise. [T]
- **should · Don't touch statues or ritual objects** unless invited. [T]
- **nice · Goshuin book.** A brush-written stamp from each temple, about ¥300–500. Buy the book at the first temple. [K]
- **nice · At Kiyomizu-dera's Otowa waterfall, drink from one stream only.** Drinking from all three is seen as greedy. [K]

## Trains

- **must · No phone calls; ringer on silent.** [N]
- **should · No eating on local trains.** Eating on the Shinkansen is normal, so bring an ekiben. [N]
- **should · Let people off first and queue at the floor markings.** [N]
- **should · Backpack on your front in crowds.** [T]
- **should · Escalators: stand left in Tokyo, right in Osaka.** Kyoto is mixed, so copy the people ahead of you. [N]
- **nice · Women-only cars** are marked in pink, usually the first or last car at rush hour. [N]

## Taxis

- **should · Don't touch the door.** The driver opens and closes it. [T]
- **nice · Show the address card.** Don't try to pronounce it. [V]

## Shops

- **should · Keep every receipt and your passport handy** for tax-free. See `money-and-shopping.md`. [N]
- **nice · Bags cost a few yen.** Say "fukuro wa irimasen" if you don't need one. [V]
